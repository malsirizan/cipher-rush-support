Privacy Policy

Cipher Rush

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cipher Rush handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Cipher Rush does not require an account and does not currently collect personal information through a developer-operated server.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay information locally on your device, such as puzzle progress, settings, hints, scores, and streaks. This information is used to provide and restore game functionality. Removing the app may remove locally stored information, depending on your device and backup settings.

Support communications

If you contact support by email, we receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and device or app details. We use that information only to respond to your request, troubleshoot problems, and improve the app.

Advertising, analytics, and purchases

The current version covered by this policy does not use a third-party advertising or analytics service that collects information from users. If advertising, analytics, cloud accounts, or additional purchase services are added later, this policy and the app's App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released.

Children's privacy

Cipher Rush is not intended to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account or collect personal information through a developer-operated server, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Parents or guardians may contact us with questions.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay information can generally be removed by using any available reset feature in the app or by deleting the app. Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, prevent abuse, or satisfy legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information submitted through support communications. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email cipherrushsupport@icloud.com.
